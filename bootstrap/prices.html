Prices

One purchase, two things: time on the site and points for the cafe.

A pass opens every game in the games list for as long as it lasts, and adds cafe points to your account straight away. There is no second price list. Games are never sold separately, and treats in the virtual cafe are paid for in points, never in tenge.

Passes

Prices are in tenge. Points are added once, at the start of the pass, and stay on the account even after the time runs out.

Pass options, updated September 2026.
Plan Time Price Cafe points Includes
Hour Pass 1 hour 700 KZT 40 points All six games, one saved game slot
Evening Pass 5 hours, after 18:00 1 900 KZT 120 points All six games, group games for up to 12 people
Day Pass 12 hours 3 500 KZT 300 points Everything in the Evening Pass, plus unlimited saved games
Student Pass 30 days, 2 hours a day 9 900 KZT 600 points Everything above, with a student card checked once at sign-up

Good to know

  • Time only counts while you play. The clock runs when a game is open and stops when you close it, so reading the rules costs you nothing.
  • Unused time expires with the pass. An Hour Pass is an hour from the moment it starts; time left at the end is not carried into the next one.
  • Points do not expire. They sit on the account until you spend them, long after the pass they came with has finished.
  • One account, one pass at a time. Buying a new pass while one is running adds its points immediately and its time afterwards.

Payment is not part of this stage of the project. The table above describes the plans; buying one is added together with the accounts system in a later stage.
