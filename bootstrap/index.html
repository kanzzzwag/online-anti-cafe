Buy time, not snacks

An online anti-cafe works on one simple idea: you pay for the minutes you spend here, and everything inside is already yours. A pass opens every game on the site and fills your account with cafe points. Spend those points in the virtual cafe on a coffee, a slice of pizza or a cookie, and keep it beside your avatar or send it to a friend.

Browse games See passes

How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve a waiter.

  1. Step 1

    Buy a pass

    Pick an hour, an evening or a whole day. The pass sets how much time you get and how many cafe points land on your account.

  2. Step 2

    Play any game

    Every title on the site is included. Nothing is locked behind a second payment, and your time only runs while a game is open.

  3. Step 3

    Spend your points

    Order a treat in the virtual cafe. It appears next to your avatar, and you can pass it to a friend instead of keeping it.

Why players like it

  • One pass opens everything

    There is no separate price for a game, a board or a deck. Buying time is the only purchase on the site.

  • Points come with the pass

    Every plan adds cafe points to your account, so the virtual cafe is never a second bill.

  • Treats can be given away

    A coffee you ordered can be sent to a friend, which is how most people here say thank you for a good game.

  • Nothing expires quietly

    Unused points stay on the account for as long as it exists, and the balance is always shown before you order.

Everything you see here is virtual. The cafe exists on the screen, beside your avatar, and a treat you order stays inside your account.
