House rules

Short, fair and the same for everyone who comes in.

A real anti-cafe hangs its rules on the wall by the door. This one keeps them here. There are not many of them, and most exist so that a group game on one shared screen stays fun for the whole table.

Your time

The clock runs only while you play

Time on a pass is counted from the moment a game opens to the moment it closes. Reading the rules of a game, browsing the cafe menu or changing your avatar costs nothing.

One pass at a time

Buying a new pass while another is running adds its points straight away and queues its time after the current one. Nothing is lost, and nothing is counted twice.

Your points

Points belong to the account

Cafe points arrive with a pass and stay on the account after the pass ends. They are spent only in the virtual cafe and can never be exchanged back into money.

Gifts are final

A treat sent to a friend arrives beside their avatar at once. It cannot be taken back, so check the name before you press the button.

Playing with other people

Pass the device, not the answers

Hidden role games deal each role on the same screen, one person at a time. Look away while somebody else reads theirs, and hand the device on without comment.

Be kind at the table

Arguing about a quiz answer is part of the fun; arguing with a person is not. Anyone who spoils a game for others can have their account paused by the team.
