The virtual cafe

Where cafe points turn into something warm on the screen.

Every pass you buy adds cafe points to your account, and this is the only place they are spent. A treat you order is virtual: it appears next to your avatar with the time you ordered it, and it stays there until you replace it. Nothing ever leaves the screen, and no treat costs real money on top of your pass.

Treats can also be given away. Order one, choose a friend instead of yourself, and it arrives beside their avatar with your name on it. It is the usual way people here say thank you after a long game of werewolf.

How points work

  • Points arrive with the pass. Each plan on the prices page lists exactly how many points it carries, and they land on the account the moment the pass starts.
  • Treats are virtual. An order changes what sits beside your avatar and nothing else. The whole cafe lives inside your account.
  • Points can be gifted. Any treat can be sent to another account instead of your own, and the sender's name travels with it.
  • Unused points stay. They do not expire when the pass does, so a small balance left over from Tuesday is still there on Friday.

The Order buttons are placeholders in this stage. Each treat card already carries the name and the cost of its treat in the markup, so the working cafe can read them directly later.
